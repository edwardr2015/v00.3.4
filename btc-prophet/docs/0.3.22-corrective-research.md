## Market and settlement

The provider is the Polymarket US production gateway, `gateway.polymarket.us`. It selects BTC five-minute UP/DOWN contracts with index symbol BRTI and validates the market status and actual timestamps. Observed slugs use `cpc-btc-updown-5m-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMMz`. The US metadata provides `priceToBeat`, `settlementPrice`, and the settlement window. The official settlement endpoint publishes the long-side payout. The observed implementation scores a tie as UP and checks official resolved sides when the payout endpoint is absent. BTC spot is for display and cannot settle a market. The panel's introductory copy describes a 60-second reference average. The metadata and payout audit verifies published values and does not reconstruct the benchmark's underlying average or independently exercise a tie.

A sample of 25 Report 5 contracts matched the US metadata and official payout endpoint after respecting the retry delay on HTTP 429 responses. The workbook lists every sampled slug. The linked open-source projects relate to public research and do not establish compatibility with this US contract. Keep the US provider and its own contract fields as the source of truth. ## Additional shadow forecasting and Trade Quality

The existing primary already uses volatility, momentum and depth when modeling execution and can consult a regime-conditioned comparison ranking. Older research includes optional regime/HMM and sequence paths. The new +90-second panel evaluates simple EWMA volatility, a three-degree Student-t gap forecast, scalar Kalman-filtered trend, top-five displayed-depth imbalance, and nearest-neighbor predictions from earlier verified markets. Displayed imbalance is a proxy for liquidity. It does not provide the initiator of executed trades. Dedicated GARCH, jump-diffusion fitting and genuine multi-exchange divergence are deferred pending sufficient synchronized data and an untouched evaluation plan. Existing standbys can publish fallback spot, but a fallback value alone cannot establish venue divergence.

Trade Quality is a separate counterfactual gate. It evaluates one directional estimate with an interval based on earlier verified comparable rounds, an executable standard and stressed $5 fill, liquidity, price, spread and disagreement. It logs WAIT and its reason as it evaluates. A window without a qualifying trade is a skip. The initial confidence and edge thresholds are research settings. A benefit on new verified rounds remains unmeasured for both the five methods and the quality gate.

The public external repositories describe different data, including global Polymarket and Chainlink-oriented methods. No external dataset is imported. A future import needs a separate venue, benchmark and license audit.
