# 0.3.23 paper research

This release carries forward the 0.3.22 research directive. It preserves the original primary strategy and balances. A research worker records a matched +240-second sale trial for Reference Gap Agreement. It evaluates only full executable paper sales on quotes confirmed after entry, within an eight-second observation window. If an executable exit is absent, that comparison holds to the official settlement. Quick and Full Excel include the comparison.

Quick Excel now lists the actual stored Selective 90 blockers by stage and provides a separate Entry Pricing Audit. The pricing audit distinguishes displayed ask, executed paper average before fees, and all-in cost per contract. The 40–70¢ and 50–70¢ columns classify the before-fee execution price. Rows retain their market identifiers so different model accounts on a round can be matched instead of treated as independent observations. Earlier releases that did not capture a quote or rejection reason remain blank or marked as missing.

Full Excel includes individual-model Brier scores and log loss, reliability buckets, and pairwise wrong-direction correlation. Model correlations use one shared verified outcome per market for the +90-second panel. Constant error series have no defined correlation. These are diagnostic estimates and cannot validate a 90% win target.

The Polymarket US crypto FAQ confirms that BTC Up/Down uses the arithmetic mean of the 60 one-second BRTI values strictly before the opening and closing timestamps. Both endpoint averages are rounded to cents. An equal close and open settles UP. Data gaps defer settlement. An official live tie example has not been independently exercised. [Polymarket US crypto FAQ](https://docs.polymarket.us/faqs/crypto-faqs)

The exact 142- and 19-trade cohorts still require the original records or identifiers. GARCH, jump diffusion, and venue divergence need suitable archived data and an independent evaluation plan. Forward profitability, Trade Quality uplift, and any 90% strategy remain unvalidated. The 200-trade research checkpoint is independent of app startup.

The public historical candle series was checked separately. For five sampled markets with 60 complete one-second candles at each boundary, averaging the published candle closes did not reproduce any of the ten official opening and closing price levels. All five reconstructed directions matched, and the separate official payout audit matched 25 of 25. The reason for the price-level discrepancy is unresolved. Use the published market opening, closing reference and payout for replay labels. The archived candle closes should not be used to regenerate those reference levels. The separate research workbook includes each discrepancy.

The +240-second historical shadow underperformed holding on the same eligible Report 9 replay entries. At standard costs it sold 15 of 29 positions and lost an additional $1.05. In the higher-cost case it sold 15 of 28 and lost an additional $2.24 relative to holding. This isolated comparison does not change the active exit policy.
