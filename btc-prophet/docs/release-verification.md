## Interpreting the evidence

Smoke tests and stress results are synthetic. A separate read-only feed probe tests public integrations. The archived sequence-only walk-forward scored 8,568 rounds after warm-up. The sequence component scored Brier 0.2631 and log loss 0.7219. The simple frequency baseline scored 0.2504 and 0.6939. Lower is better. This result favors the baseline in that archive and does not establish a result for the complete odds/price blend or for profit. Preserve it as an unfavorable research result.

No candidate is ready for operational acceptance until the Intel iMac preflight and the requested monitoring and forward experiments are complete.
